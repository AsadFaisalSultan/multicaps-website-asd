Innovation-driven design

Engineered for high-speed filling

Our capsule geometry is designed for secure closure, clean separation and consistent performance on semi-automatic and fully automatic filling machines.

  1. Snap-fit groove

    A groove on the cap locks firmly to the body after filling, preventing leakage of the contents.

  2. Cone-cut body

    A tapered body rim guides the cap on, making closing easy and avoiding locking failures and telescoping.

  3. Pre-lock notches

    Hold cap and body together during transport and feeding, then release cleanly in the filling machine.

  4. Twin air vents

    Two vents let air escape on closing, keeping internal pressure even and protecting shells in storage and on high-speed machines.
