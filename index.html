Tour the Facility

From raw gelatin to your filling line

Eight controlled stages, each with its own quality checkpoint, so every shipment performs the same as the last.

Stage 1 of 8

Raw material sourcing

We buy halal-certified, TSE/BSE-free pharmaceutical gelatin and edible food-grade colours only from approved, audited suppliers. Every lot arrives with full documentation.

Stage 2 of 8

Incoming quality testing

Each lot of gelatin and colourant is sampled and tested by our QC laboratory, and released for production only when it meets specification.

Stage 3 of 8

Gelatin melting

Released gelatin is dissolved and conditioned in jacketed stainless-steel tanks, then blended with colours to the exact viscosity the dipping line needs.

Stage 4 of 8

Capsule forming

Stainless-steel pins dip into the gelatin solution, then the film is dried, stripped, trimmed and joined into matched caps and bodies.

Stage 5 of 8

Printing

Logos, names and product codes are printed in edible pharmaceutical ink using axial, spin or multicolour printing, to your approved artwork.

Stage 6 of 8

Inspection

PLC-controlled lines with high-resolution cameras and laser sensors check every capsule and automatically remove any with defects.

Stage 7 of 8

Packing & storage

Approved capsules are sealed and cartoned, then held under controlled temperature and humidity so they reach you in perfect condition.

Stage 8 of 8

Dispatch

Orders leave the plant with their batch documentation, for customers across Pakistan and in export markets.

Frequently asked questions

Can't find what you need? Our sales team can help with specifications, stock and lead times.

Contact us
How can we verify the capsules are halal?
We provide a copy of our current Halal certificate with your quotation or on request. Our gelatin is sourced from halal-certified, TSE/BSE-free suppliers and documented lot by lot.
Which colours can we order?
Any colour can be matched to your requirement, in clear transparent, coloured transparent, opaque or pearl finishes. Share a Pantone reference or a physical sample and we will match it.
Do you keep ready stock?
Yes. More than 300 colours are held in all sizes and are available for immediate delivery.
What can be printed on a capsule?
Anything your product requires: company logo, brand name, product name or code. We offer axial, spin and multicolour printing.
How should empty capsules be stored?
Store in the original sealed packaging at 15–25 °C and 45–65% relative humidity, away from direct sunlight and heat sources.
Do you supply outside Pakistan?
Yes. We serve customers across Pakistan and supply pharmaceutical manufacturers internationally. Contact our sales team for export enquiries.

Planning your next capsule order?
