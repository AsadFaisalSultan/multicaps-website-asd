Privacy policy

This policy explains what information we collect through multicaps.net and how we use it.

Information we collect

We collect the information you choose to give us through our contact, sample request and job application forms. This may include your name, company, email address, phone number, address, country, product requirements and, for job applications, your CV.

How we use it

We use your information only to respond to your enquiry, prepare samples and quotations, process job applications and maintain our business relationship with you. We do not sell your personal information.

Third-party services

This website loads fonts from Google Fonts and embeds maps from Google Maps and videos from YouTube (only when you choose to play them). These services may collect technical information such as your IP address under their own privacy policies.

Data retention

We keep enquiry and application information only as long as needed for the purpose it was collected, or as required by law.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you by emailing info@multicaps.net.
