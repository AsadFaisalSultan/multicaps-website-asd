Quality at every stage

Quality is built in, not inspected in. These are the checkpoints every Multicaps batch passes through.

Incoming

Raw material release

Every lot of gelatin and colourant is sampled, tested and approved by our QC laboratory against specification and supplier documentation before it reaches production.

In-process

Process monitoring

Viscosity, film thickness, moisture and dimensions are monitored during production so drift is caught early, not at the end of the batch.

Inspection

Automated inspection

PLC-controlled lines with high-resolution cameras and laser sensors inspect every capsule and remove defects automatically.

Release

Finished product testing

Finished batches are tested and released by QA, then packed and stored under controlled temperature and humidity until dispatch.

An in-house QC laboratory

Our laboratory is equipped with analytical instruments, stability chambers and drying ovens, staffed by experienced quality personnel.

  • Moisture control

    Shell moisture is kept within the optimal range for flexibility without brittleness.

  • Dimensional accuracy

    Length, diameter and wall thickness checked for reliable machine performance.

  • Stability testing

    Controlled-climate chambers verify performance over shelf life.
