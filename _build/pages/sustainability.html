Corporate social responsibility

Our mission goes beyond manufacturing capsule shells. We are equally committed to a positive impact on the environment and the communities we serve.

{{icon:sun}} Renewable energy

A step towards sustainability

We have adopted solar energy at our manufacturing facilities. By installing solar panels we harness renewable power and reduce our dependence on non-renewable sources.

This investment lowers our carbon footprint and greenhouse gas emissions, conserves natural resources and improves energy efficiency across our operations. It reflects our belief that responsible manufacturing and care for the environment go hand in hand.

{{icon:leaf}} Plantation drive

Planting today for a greener tomorrow

In line with our aim to create a positive impact beyond healthcare, we launched a plantation drive focused on nurturing green spaces and countering the effects of climate change.

Through this effort we aim to restore ecological balance, support biodiversity and contribute to cleaner air and healthier surroundings for the generations to come.

Businesses must play an active role in protecting the planet. We are proud to be part of the solution, planting the seeds today for a sustainable and thriving future.
