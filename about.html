Pioneers in capsule manufacturing in Pakistan

Multicaps is a specialised manufacturer of halal empty hard gelatin capsule shells. We make quality capsules so our customers can bring quality medicines to market.

Get to know us

For nearly five decades our group has been part of Pakistan's capsule industry. Today Multicaps operates the country's largest facility for empty hard gelatin capsules, producing 3.24 billion shells a year in sizes 00 to 4.

We combine modern equipment with strict quality control at every step, and we support our customers before and after every sale with technical guidance on filling, storage and specification.

Our growth

Growth is the constant pursuit of becoming the best version of ourselves.

  1. 1975Where we started

    A modest shop in Kachi Gali

    Born out of vision and a passion for excellence, a small shop in Kachi Gali, Karachi, marked the beginning of what would become a pioneer of Pakistan's capsule industry.

  2. TodayWhere we are now

    Pakistan's largest capsule facility

    Multicaps is the biggest facility in Pakistan manufacturing empty hard gelatin capsules in a full range of sizes, serving more than 450 customers and supplying nearly 80% of the national pharmaceutical industry.

  3. NextWhere we aspire to be

    A global capsule partner

    Having established our strength at home, we are expanding into international markets with the aim of becoming a trusted global supplier.
