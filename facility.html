Capsule production

Our production process is a carefully controlled blend of art and science. From the selection of premium raw materials to dipping, drying and trimming, we hold the highest levels of precision and consistency.

Advanced machinery and an experienced team give us the capacity to produce capsules in every standard size and in hundreds of colours, around the clock.

Capsule inspection

Our inspection lines combine programmable logic controllers (PLC), high-resolution cameras and laser sensors to examine every capsule for defects.

  • High-resolution cameras

    Capture even the smallest imperfections in colour, shape and print.

  • Laser sensors

    Detect dimensional defects and trigger automatic removal of rejected shells.

Quality control laboratory

Advanced analytical equipment and experienced quality personnel check raw materials, in-process samples and finished capsules.

Quality & compliance

Production flow

Each stage has its own quality checkpoint before material moves to the next.

Stage 1 of 8

Raw material sourcing

We buy halal-certified, TSE/BSE-free pharmaceutical gelatin and edible food-grade colours only from approved, audited suppliers. Every lot arrives with full documentation.

Stage 2 of 8

Incoming quality testing

Each lot of gelatin and colourant is sampled and tested by our QC laboratory, and released for production only when it meets specification.

Stage 3 of 8

Gelatin melting

Released gelatin is dissolved and conditioned in jacketed stainless-steel tanks, then blended with colours to the exact viscosity the dipping line needs.

Stage 4 of 8

Capsule forming

Stainless-steel pins dip into the gelatin solution, then the film is dried, stripped, trimmed and joined into matched caps and bodies.

Stage 5 of 8

Printing

Logos, names and product codes are printed in edible pharmaceutical ink using axial, spin or multicolour printing, to your approved artwork.

Stage 6 of 8

Inspection

PLC-controlled lines with high-resolution cameras and laser sensors check every capsule and automatically remove any with defects.

Stage 7 of 8

Packing & storage

Approved capsules are sealed and cartoned, then held under controlled temperature and humidity so they reach you in perfect condition.

Stage 8 of 8

Dispatch

Orders leave the plant with their batch documentation, for customers across Pakistan and in export markets.
